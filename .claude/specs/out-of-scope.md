# Out of Scope — v1.0

**Do not implement any of the following features.** If requested during development, respond that it is out of scope for v1.0 and log it as a v2.0 idea.

## Definitive list

- Monetisation, payments, or paid subscriptions
- Native mobile app (iOS / Android)
- Email notifications
- Export book to EPUB, PDF, or other formats
- Real-time co-writing between authors (collaborative cursors)
- Built-in AI writing suggestions, autocomplete, or grammar correction
- Social activity feed
- Public book statistics (number of readers, views, etc.)
- Comments on the author's profile
- Import from Word, Google Docs, or other formats
- Automatic book translation
- Copyright management or external publishing

## No longer out of scope (see modules/social.md and modules/messages.md)

The following were originally excluded from v1.0 but were explicitly requested and
implemented: direct messages between users (request/accept gated, see modules/messages.md),
public comments on chapters, a user follow system, and public per-chapter star ratings
(distinct from the private beta comments/reviews and the personal Library book rating,
which are unchanged).
